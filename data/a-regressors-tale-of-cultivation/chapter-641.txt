"I don't know either."

"Someday...someday. I will give you a name that fits you perfectly. I promise."

"..."

"...Shall we go in?"

"I will see it with my own eyes. Let's go."

A massive alchemical furnace.

Both he and I have reached the realm of Earth Upper Immortal.

"...Oh...Hye...Seo...!"

Oh Hyun-seok howls as he looks at the pill.

"Oh Hye-seo...!!!"

Seo Hweol's bones.

And Oh Hye-seo herself...

Shwiririririk...

The appearance of her gently caressing Seo Hweol's bones, after personally building the Sea Dragon Palace where she once lived together with Seo Hweol.

The appearance of her continuously calling Seo Hweol's name, seeking him again and again.

She was insane.

Ten thousand years.

She was mad.

A hundred thousand years.

She firmly believed Seo Hweol would come back to life.

Ten million years.

—Seo Eun-hyun is my enemy, and my greatest adversary. Because...Seo Eun-hyun was Seo Hweol's greatest adversary. So even if I cannot defeat him...someday, when he returns to life, together with him, gain the starting point to bring Seo Eun-hyun down.

Seo Hweol's greatest adversary.

"From today, you are my immediate disciple. You said you learned the Confucian Family Method, right? You will need social authority. "Now, this is the specialty of the Purple Gold Realm, called fire-grilled sea cucumber."

"Th-This is..."

Makli Hyun-ah swallows his saliva as he looks at something like a lump made of black energy.

"Isn't it a ghostly creature?"

Makli Hyun-ah hesitates as he looks at this black-colored lump that wriggles little by little despite being grilled over direct fire, but when he closes his eyes and eats it, a truly tremendous taste strikes Makli Hyun-ah's soul far beyond what he expects.

'Is it because it is a ghost creature...? It feels like it stimulates taste on the soul itself... What in the world is this taste...!?'

At the taste of the fire-grilled sea cucumber, a specialty of the Purple Gold Realm, Makli Hyun-ah can't even open his mouth and only marvels, and Makli Hweol, who is invited together as Makli Hyun-ah's close friend, also tastes the fire-grilled sea cucumber and, as if something comes to mind, closes his eyes and starts savoring the flavor.

It is then,

Chiiiiiik...

"Huh?"

Makli Hyun-ah suddenly sees his own hand melting away.

"Wait, what is th-ueooooo...eoooooook..."

Terrified, Makli Hyun-ah tries to rise from his seat, but he is horrified as he watches his entire body, starting from his vocal cords, melt down black.

However, Ban Ta laughs heartily and comforts Makli Hyun-ah.

"Do not worry. If you want to become even better, you need to throw away what was there originally. The fire-grilled sea cucumber of the Purple Gold Realm, among them, the black fire-grilled sea cucumber that is praised as the supreme grade, causes complete transformation to happen immediately when you eat it."

Makli Hyun-ah, feeling dumbfounded, asks through mental speech.

[Does this thing called complete transformation originally mean melting away the body that was there and making it anew?]

"Of course! Do not worry. The newly made parts will become sturdier. Using your body as the material, the fire-grilled sea cucumber gifts you an even more perfected body, so it doesn't stop being your body."

As if those words are true, Makli Hyun-ah's arms and vocal cords begin to sprout again from within the black liquid.

"...! My skin... Huh, my voice too!?"

And Makli Hyun-ah notices that the skin of both his arms, his tendons and bones, and his throat voice are much clearer.

"A fire-grilled sea cucumber of ordinary quality takes a little time for the old body to melt down and the new body to sprout, but the black fire-grilled sea cucumber is different. The moment you eat it, your body's balance is set right, and a fleshly body optimized to accept perfect primeval energy is born."

"Th-There's such a thing...? Uh, but then why does Makli Hweol have no reaction at all?"

"Hm? Hmm..."

At Makli Hyun-ah's question, Ban Ta stares fixedly at Makli Hweol.

"This guy..."

After starting at Makli Hweol for a while, Ban Ta frowns and speaks.

"The black fire-grilled sea cucumber reconstructs the body only for people who have parts with problems in their body."

"...? Then did my body have a problem?"

"That is right. No matter what, a kid who doesn't learn Immortal Family Methods will have the balance of the Taiji and the microcosm inside the body broken, so there should be many problems. In other words, that fellow's understanding of the Immortal Family Method is extremely high, so as he raised his realm, he himself perfectly straightened out every imbalance inside his body by himself..."

Suspicion rises in Ban Ta's eyes.

"But is such talent naturally possible? He is at the half-step Four-Axis stage, but even so, he's still only at the Heavenly Being stage..."

"Hmm... Well, is he really an incredible genius? Will you not become my disciple too?"

"Hoho...I am grateful for the praise, but for now I want to wander around unaffiliated and broaden my knowledge."

"Hmm, well... Normally, I would have tried to recruit you into Ban Ta Castle, but today is the day I received a direct disciple, so I will allow it with a broad heart."

Thinking it over, Ban Ta decides that Makli Hweol does not seem suspicious, and continues enjoying the feast.

At the violent flavor of the fire-grilled sea cucumber, Makli Hyun-ah stuffs fire-grilled sea cucumbers into his mouth, and Ban Ta watches that sight and smiles with satisfaction.

"You eat so heartily, disciple. Still, eat slowly. If you overconsume, as a side effect, the reconstruction time for some parts can become sluggish."

"Th-Thank y—hheup!"

Makli Hyun-ah keeps chewing and swallowing the violent taste of the fire-grilled sea cucumbers amid the strange sensation of his body melting away in places and newly sprouting again.

'W-Was my body really this full of problems...? This is like my whole body is being reborn anew... Is it because I didn't learn Immortal Family Methods that I have this many problems...?'

Tudududuk, tududuk!

While Makli Hyun-ah keeps putting the wriggling black lumps into his mouth, he suddenly feels something strange in his chest.

"Uh."

"Is there some problem, Daoist?"

Makli Hweol looks at Makli Hyun-ah's expression and asks in puzzlement, and Makli Hyun-ah, with a slightly stiff expression, rubs his chest.

"...My nipples...melted away, but they aren't being reconstructed..."

"Hoho...pohuhup..."

At those words, Makli Hweol, who always keeps his way of laughing unified to just one, can't hold it in and turns his head away, and Ban Ta laughs heartily and comforts Makli Hyun-ah.

"See, what do I tell you? If you overconsume fire grilled sea cucumber, I said there will be side effects. Ahahahaha! Do not worry. The reconstruction time just slows down; in the end, it will reconstruct again."

"Ah...I see. Thank you."

With this unpleasant side effect, Makli Hyun-ah stares at the last lump of fire-grilled sea cucumber placed on his plate.

'It's not like nipples matter whether they regenerate slowly or not... And Master says it's slow to reconstruct, but in the end it becomes normal again... So isn't it fine if I eat one more?'

As Makli Hyun-ah recalls the violent taste of the fire-grilled sea cucumber, his hand trembles, and in the end, he can't endure it and brings the fire-grilled sea cucumber to his mouth one more time.

'After eating just this, I'll stop. Still, I have to at least eat everything I put on my plate.'

And finally, when Makli Hyun-ah swallows the last fire-grilled sea cucumber in full.

"...Huh?"

Makli Hyun-ah suddenly gets a hollow feeling down below.

He goes blank for a moment, stares downward with dazed eyes, then looks at Ban Ta.

"M-Master...?"

"Mm? Hahahaha!"

Ban Ta looks at Makli Hyun-ah, realizes what has happened to him, and laughs loudly.

"Do not worry too much. Like I said again, it's only that the reconstruction period has grown long from overconsumption. As time passes, the molting sea cucumber will reconstruct all the defects and imbalances of your body. With your realm, you can shoot through spatial transmission anyway, so isn't it fine even if you don't have a hole like that?"

"No...it is possible, but... No, still..."

With what had been there now gone, Makli Hyun-ah feels an extreme emptiness, and at last he breaks free from the addiction to the violent taste of the fire-grilled sea cucumber.

'This is dangerous. Now I really have to stop eating it...'

Since the other foods at the banquet are also delicious, Makli Hyun-ah tastes the other foods while cold sweat streams from his forehead.

Like so, Makli Hyun-ah spends a day within a banquet that begins at first with the tremendous gourmet pleasure of new food, but in the latter half leaves him fretting anxiously while minding the organs that disappeared.

The next day arrives.

Summoned to a training ground in Ban Ta Castle, Makli Hyun-ah goes through the procedure of directly receiving proof from Ban Ta that he belongs to Ban Ta Castle.

"This is the seal that proves your affiliation with Ban Ta Castle. Stamp it onto the ascension plaque."

"Thank you, Master..."

'So this is the not-recommended method Ancestor Makli Cheon-sa spoke of.'

'Aside from that, Ancestor Makli Cheon-sa said it's foolish to become the disciple of Sacred Master Ban Ta... Tsk. I don't know if it's really to that extent.'

Paaat!

"Ah, that is..."

"Disciple. How much do you know about the social authority you handle?"

At Ban Ta's question, Makli Hyun-ah thinks it over carefully and ponders.

And he is able to realize one thing.

'I have never thought about the nature of social power until now...'

"...Enough. The story has gone off track. Anyway, that isn't what's important. You must understand well how the structure of this world is formed to understand the source of powers well, and understanding that source will help you perform your method going forward."

"Yes, I understand."

"In this world there is something called the Three Planes. Do you understand planes?"

"Yes, I roughly understand. If the height of water is called the water level, then it's a term that comes from asking how you express the height differences of dimensions..."

"Right. As you know, in this world there are three large planes. The Plane of Fate. The Plane of Qi. The Plane of Soul. These are also said to be the planes corresponding to Heaven, Earth, and Heart, respectively."

Ban Ta's explanation continues.

"Standard Immortal Family Methods aim to start from the Plane of Qi and rise sequentially up to the Plane of Fate. Whether you build up in the Plane of Qi or pull in the Plane of Fate is where the divide between the Earth Tribe and the Heaven Tribe lies, but there is no change in the fact that you rise sequentially. But the Confucian Family Method and Naming Family Method you learn are the opposite."

"The opposite, meaning...?"

"The power of the Naming Family takes as its foundation that fate is contained in the name, and it is a method that makes you begin cultivation from the Plane of Fate."

"Pardon...?"

Makli Hyun-ah gapes his mouth at the scale of the method, which is more immense than he expected.

'No, that's possible?'

"Up until now, you most likely cultivated by gathering and accumulating energy into names...but the might of the Naming Family Method lies in that, through a [name], you can directly distort and twist fate."

"E-Even though it's a method you can only cultivate up to the Core Formation stage?"

Ban Ta steadily explains to Makli Hyun-ah about the beings each method takes as its foundation.

"The basic Immortal Family Method takes as its foundation the One Qi of the Primordial Chaos and the Immeasurable Life Buddha. Before that...um, you don't need to know this. Anyway, as much as they take their foundation in the world's fundamental beings, they are powerful and universal.

“Next, the second most universal Heart Dao Blooming takes as its foundation the One Qi of the Primordial Chaos and the being called the Golden Speed.

'What kind of octopus arms are those?'

"In the case of the Eloquence Family Method, it is a bizarre method that, based on illusion techniques, deals with adjusting roles that match a story, and it takes as its foundation a being called the [Owner of Liberation].

'So the Naming Family Method uses the power of the Plane of Fate, and the Confucian Family Method uses the power of the Plane of Soul...'

"Then the Confucian Family Method's suppression of Heaven and Earth spiritual energy...is it ultimately a phenomenon where the Plane of Soul suppresses the phenomena of the Plane of Qi?"

"Yes, your understanding is excellent! What a clever kid you are...!"

Ban Ta praises Makli Hyun-ah lavishly and ruffles his hair.

'Naming Dao Conferring Gods Canon is Fate, Pacifying Myriad Demons Under Heaven Survey is Soul...and Canvas of Myriad Forms and Star Genesis is, as a Shaman Family Method...'

In other words, if Makli Hyun-ah only learns the methods assigned to him that symbolize the Plane of Fate, the Plane of Soul, and the Plane of Qi, then the difficulty of Makli Hyun-ah piercing through the Three Planes of Qi-Soul-Fate drops exponentially.

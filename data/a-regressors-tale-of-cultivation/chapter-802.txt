'This world is a tomb, and a cradle.'

Those beings are omniscient and omnipotent in their respective worlds, create countless worlds, and can do and become anything within them. They are the very truth that knows every conclusion and causality of what happens in those worlds.

They create their own separate worlds and live in those worlds.

Those separate worlds can influence one another and be similar, or can be completely different worlds.

And...

For such Creator Gods, death does not exist. It is only a change in form.

Because death does not exist, there also do not exist parents or a source-like being that gives birth to them.

There is effect but no cause.

At some moment they simply are born, and at some moment they create worlds and exist.

However, though birth does not exist, the basis on which they are born exists.

Cradle of Chaos, Conception Palace of Omnipotence, Gestation world of the Creator Gods-there are numerous names, but one thing is certain.

There exists something like a cradle in the shape of an egg, filled with the medium of creation, chaos, and the Creator Gods always are born within it and make worlds of their own.

And...

The worlds within this chaos where Hong Fan and the Chief Gods are born are also such a place.

However, unlike other gestation worlds, this gestation world has something that can be called 'causality'.

'A predecessor Creator God...exists.'

They intended to make a different Heaven and Earth.

A separate world where any being can become complete.

A place where there exists a true answer to what life is.

But in the end, that is only the Creator God's own answer.

The Creator God sought a fundamental truth that holds no matter what world they go to.

Therefore, they sacrificed themselves.

They collapsed their own body and authority and turned this world back to the gestation-world period.

They refined their own soul through a brazier that a great divinity among their creations, comparable to themselves, made and dedicated when leaving the world.

Through the three legs of the brazier, the soul was split into three parts.

Thus, the Creator God made with their own soul the Three Daos.

The great Daos called Fate, History, and Miracle.

And through those Daos, worlds were born even in the primeval gestation period, and living beings were born and able to live.

They refined their soul into three parts and perished, leaving a single strand of continuance.

By present standards, one would say they cut off and left behind a single thread of consciousness.

-Find the answer.

They do not want to believe that what they dictate is the answer.

An answer that holds wherever one goes.

To find it, they thought absolute beings of the same rank must constantly put their heads together and debate and discuss, sometimes opposing one another and sometimes embracing one another as they wrestle.

Something that blossoms within the dialogues and stories between one another.

They believed that is greater than a truth that a single absolute being determines.

-Refine your rank, grow your existence, and reach the Three Daos.

The consciousness of the great being thus issued a decree.

All living beings of this world were to become stronger and stronger, gain divinity, become transcendents, and obtain the qualification to challenge the Three Daos.

However, obtaining all Three Daos and becoming a Creator God-

-Let a single existence grasp only one Dao and become an absolute being of Quasi-Creator rank, so that the three absolute ones forever embrace each other and at times oppose one another...thus, someday within the stories of the three beings, the truth will be brought forth.

The true truth that blossoms within the stories of three equal absolute beings.

That is what the predecessor Creator God hoped and planned for.

The answer of a true truth that holds no matter what world one goes to.

Starting with the first Chief God, Brightness, the Chief Gods of Water, Fire, Earth, Wood, and Metal were born within the single strand of consciousness left by the Creator God.

In other words, the Chief Gods who governed the universe were in effect the direct children of the Creator God.

They, bearing the Creator God's will on their backs, devoted each of their lives to seize an even more exalted rank, grasp the Three Daos, and manifest a Heaven that puts forth their own answers.

The answer.

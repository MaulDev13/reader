Chapter 631: Great Net Advancement

Kugugugung!

The advancement to Great Net Immortal proceeds as follows:

First, one transmits the power of their Immortal Dao toward the worlds where they have either destroyed or created civilizations.

Then, toward 'all' worlds that possess civilizations in each Heavenly Domain.

Through those phenomena, one simultaneously elevates the cultivation of all living beings in the Heavenly Domains connected to them.

If the Immortal Dao of the Mountain accumulates specks to build a great mountain,

And if, during this process, the power to be distributed to the Heavenly Domains where one has cultivated as an Upper Immortal becomes insufficient, the entire body is forcibly scattered and absorbed into Mount Sumeru to be devoured by the Great Mountain Supreme Deity.

This is the Great Net Immortal advancement ritual.

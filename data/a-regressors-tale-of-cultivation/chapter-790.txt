The Emptiness Supreme Deity Myeong Woon, in whom it is embedded, gives up keeping the Akashic Records and becomes shackles that tighten around Bong Hwa's neck, suppressing her power.

Kugugugugu!

Bong Hwa musters all her strength to escape those shackles, but her power is perfectly offset by the shackles made of the Emptiness Supreme Deity and she cannot even pierce the birdcage.

The birdcage and the power of the Emptiness Supreme Deity amplify from within and completely press down the Heavenly Venerable of the Underworld.

The Future King Fate Supreme Deity, with eyes whose meaning I can by no means read, looks down and mutters at the shrunken form of the sealed Bong Hwa.

"Good. Resist. Struggle."

Woo-woooooong!

He gradually floats the birdcage into the void.

"For only resisting fate...is your reason for existence, Hwe-ah."

The Absolute of History.

The Akashic Records depart from the embrace of the Emptiness Supreme Deity and also slip out of the advancement ritual of the Heavenly Venerable of the Underworld.

The authority of Omniscience, inside that Mount Sumeru, shines purely in golden radiance, under no one's control.

"I speak again."

And, before my eyes.

The Heavenly God who seized the Absolute of Heaven opens his mouth again.

"You do not need something like hope."

Kugugugugu—

The God of Light and Hope wearing that black mianguan coldly takes hope from me and prophesizes.

"I see all your moves. Do you intend to turn back time with Auspicious Soul Filling the Heavens and save your friends? I prophesize. You will not complete three thousand regressions, and I will seize the entire Severing Heaven Sword Form whole from you, and you will be killed by your own Martial Arts."

Grasping every last possibility I have hidden, he calmly prophesied.

His prophecy is neither grave nor numinous like other True Immortals.

It is merely one of those words tossed out as if speaking ordinarily.

From the start, there is no need to be sacred.

For the very standard of all sacredness is the heavens itself.

"I speak again, do not be mistaken. No matter what you all do, you are only supporting roles."

Wooooooo—

Freed completely from Kang Min-hee's constraint and my Great Mountain Pressing Down, he slowly expands once more.

A divinity so transcendent that I feel nothing at all.

That is precisely the Heavens.

"The true protagonist of the story is not you."

An existence who attained the highest Dao.

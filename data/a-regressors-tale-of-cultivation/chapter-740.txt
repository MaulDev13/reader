"Do you know about the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flags of the Northern Dipper Seven Heavenly Lords?"

After reaching the realm of Immortal Beast King and Martial Pinnacle, I tried to pull out the remaining Sealing Immortal Flags through force, but as expected of seals created to target the Vast Cold Heavenly Lord, they were made so they can never be removed by “strength' alone.

In particular, the fewer Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flags remain and the higher the realm of the one they are embedded in, the stronger their absoluteness becomes. Because of that, even now I cannot forcefully remove the last two Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flags.

“The fact that I managed to pull out the first Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag by strength alone is an immense feat.

“The conditions for the Sealing Immortal Flags that cannot be removed are as follows. One is to “defeat three Supreme Deities while at the realm of Great Net Immortal or lower." The second is to 'take the attacks of the Radiance Eight Immortals in sequence" Perhaps because the Northern Dipper Seven Heavenly Lords created this Immortal Art to target the Vast Cold Heavenly Lord, the release conditions are absurdly strict.

As it stands, I have defeated the Sal Tree Heavenly Venerable and the Heavenly Venerable of Void, and I have also participated in defeating the Heavenly Punishment Supreme Deity and the Naming Supreme Deity, summing to four Governing Immortals. Even though I have personally brought down four Governing Immortals, the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag judged that I have not purely defeated three [Supreme Deities], so the seal did not release.

“Well, this doesn't really matter.

Someday, as long as I defeat Bong Myeong, the condition of “three Supreme Deities’ will be fulfilled, so this is nothing to worry about regarding that anymore.

The problem lies with the attacks of the Radiance Eight Immortals.

“If you know a bit about this Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag, then why is it that even though I've taken the attacks of the Radiance Eight Immortals with my whole body several times, the seal still hasn't been released... Do you have any idea?"

To my question, Ji Hwa nods as if it is simple.

“I know what the problem is. I already got permission from the Northern Dipper Seven Heavenly Lords to view all the release conditions of the Northern Dipper Sealing immortal Flag. The release condition you're concerned about is..to take them [in sequence].”

Just as expected.

itis exactly the kind of troublesome condition it sounds like.

“Starting from the Great Forest Heavenly Lord, all the way to the Rain Dew Heavenly Lord. You have to take the attacks of the [Eight Radiance Supreme Deities] in the order of the Ten Heavenly Stems. That's the release condition."

However, I find myself puzzled hearing that condition.

“...I'm certain that last time, during the activation of the Salt Sea Returning Dew Jade, I took them in order..."

"No, back then the quartermaster of the Radiance Eight Immortals, the Pearl Jade Heavenly Lord, did not take part in the attack."

Thinking back to that time, I realize she is right.

I see. Indeed, after Filling the Heavens Sword Rain, the Great Sea Heavenly Lord's immortal Art came next. There was no Pearl Jade Heavenly Lord's attack.

But then, I notice something else strange.

“Wait, even setting that aside... just during the last God-Killing Spear battle...I took all of your attacks in order."

Starting from the God-Killing Spear thrown by the Great Forest Heavenly Lord, all the way to the God-Killing Spear thrown by the Rain Dew Heavenly Lord, I had taken them in sequence’.

So why is it?

At my question, Ji Hwa gives a bitter smile and speaks.

“You fought back."

"..What?"

“The final release condition of the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag... Since our Radiance Eight Immortals directly oversee its release, we heard it straight from the Northern Dipper Seven Heavenly Lords. The truth of the final Northern Dipper Sealing immortal Flag's release condition is...

“Not only taking the attacks of the Eight Owners of Light 'in sequence"...but also [without resisting]."

"...50 even if it's an attack like the God-Killing Spear..."

“Yes. In the end, calmly taking the attacks in sequence with your whole body, without resistance...that's the release condition for the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag."

I am dumbfounded, but I realize that this level of requirement is necessary to restrain an existence on the level of the Vast Cold Heavenly Lord.

"So that means...because I fought back against the God-Killing Spear in order to not die, the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag wasn't released."

“That's about right."

After a long silence, I ask Ji Hwa.

“Can't you stir things up a bit so that someday, when I stand before the Radiance Eight immortals, they all bombard me at once?"

"Do you think that would work? We've all heard that you're sealed by the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag."

In the end, even if they have me right in front of them, they might not attack in sequence at all, or even if they do attack in sequence, some of them might purposely not attack in the middle, making it all for nothing.

“Troublesome.’

I contemplate the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag's release condition of [taking the attacks of the Eight Radiance Supreme Deities in the order of the Ten Heavenly Stems without resisting] and sigh inwardly.

"Pearl Jade Heavenly Lord is the problem. Even if I manage to incite them to somehow attack you, the Pearl Jade Heavenly Lord is the quartermaster. Aside from supplementing our strength and providing the power of prophecy or blessings, they don't come to the front lines during wartime."

“That's why though cases like last time's God-Killing Spear are rare...if you had been hit by the God-Killing Spear, even you would have been sealed without a doubt... If the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag was released only for you to be sealed by us instead, that would have been even more troublesome, so there was no helping it."

I realize I am getting a headache and let out a sigh.

“...Alright. Whether I have to ask Yeong Seung or the Heavenly Venerable of Time, I'll ask them to remove it. If that doesn't work, I could always ask the Liberation Supreme Deity or the Underworld."

"Hmm... But at your level, the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag doesn't really matter anymore, does it? The power you've accumulated is far too different from the specific Vast Cold's strength that the Northern Dipper Seven Heavenly Lords intended to seal, so there shouldn't be a problem.”

“That is true."

Powers like that of the Immortal Beast King and Martial Pinnacle.

Since the powers I've accumulated are of a nature far too different from the Vast Cold Heavenly Lord's, the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag hardly affects them. However, while it cannot affect the powers themselves, it has a considerable impact on the synergy between my powers.

“The powers don't blend well

“To heal the Wheel, I have to completely unify my powers, and to do that, I must remove all the Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flags.

The Northern Dipper Sealing Immortal Flag blocks the fusion between my powers.

From the Three Great Ultimates of the Heaven Dao, Earth Dao, and Immortal Dao, to the immortal Beast King, the Ultimate of Martial Arts, and the powers of various mantras.

All of those should intertwine and naturally rotate and spin the Wheel to heal it, but the powers that do not blend instead only interfere with the Wheel's rotation.

If what is needed to turn the Wheel is pure liquid, then my current powers are not pure liquid, but a stew with various chunks floating in it.

“The unblended powers keep turning the Wheel, so it seems the overload is much greater."

If someone other than me had obtained the Wheel, I think that at least the overload would not happen this often.

As I am inwardly pondering these problems, I suddenly notice Ji Hwa holding my hand and briskly walking somewhere.

"...By the way, where are we going?"

“From now on, speak formally. I am going to place you in a Daoist temple that serves Radiance Hall.

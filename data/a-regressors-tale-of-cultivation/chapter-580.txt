As if she has something to ask me.

"What do you mean?"

"Do you have business with my subordinate? If there is a reason, please state it."

"...I failed to perceive it. At first, I thought it was simply because I had turned my attention away while focusing on you...but when you absorbed it into your body, I could tell. 'The Heavenly Venerable of the Void couldn't perceive Hong Fan?'

'A Heavenly Venerable failing to perceive an existence at the Star Shattering stage...This is far too strange...'

I clearly sense something strange.

And I read the intent that Hyeon Mu is not even trying to hide.

She undoubtedly harbors killing intent.

'She intends...to kill Hong Fan...!'

"...You plan to kill my subordinate."

"If necessary."

"In that case, I cannot show you my subordinate's face so carelessly. No matter if you are a Heavenly Venerable, that one is my subordinate. At the same time, he is someone like my family, one whom I have cared for since the moment he emerged from his egg. Even if it is you..."

Facing Hyeon Mu's killing intent, I release my own in response, tightening my grip on the Colorless Glass Sword,

"I cannot forgive anyone who lays a hand on my family!"

"..."

Hyeon Mu gazes at me in silence.

That dreadful emptiness!

Just seeing the emptiness in her eyes alone makes me feel as if my strength is being drained away.

She continues to stare at me for a moment before raising one hand.

I flinch and take a defensive stance, but she does not attack.

However, I realize what she is doing.

Ziiiiiing!

The Taiji begins to rotate.

She is reading the history of this place.

"...If you do not wish to show me, then that is fine. I can simply see for myself. Let's see... If I follow the flow of Qi backward and trace the events..."

'I cannot escape.'

I have seen her travel through the domain of purity before.

An ultimate technique that surpasses my Heavenly Escape by several levels.

If she uses that, no matter where I flee, I will never get away.

'So, if she finds Hong Fan displeasing...I must counterattack.'

That is the only way to extend both my life and Hong Fan's, even if just by a little.

I prepare myself to unleash the Phenomena Extinguishing Mantra, Great Mountain Splitting Emperor Technique, or Filling the Heavens Governing View at a moment's notice, raising my tension.

Then—

Sururuk...

The Taiji that had been spinning vanishes into the void.

'She finished reading the history...!'

But her following words are entirely unexpected.

"...Interesting. It cannot be read..."

"...!?"

With empty eyes, Hyeon Mu gives a chilling smile.

"...It could not be seen. However, I clearly read the Qi of that so-called subordinate of yours. That aura... That faint trace of history I felt from it... Huhu, huhuhuhuhu..."

"...?"

She begins to conceal her intent.

I can no longer read her intent, but somehow, I feel like I know what it is.

The True Martial Great Emperor Hyeon Mu, for some reason...is filled with rage right now.

"Hand over your subordinate. Then I will revive the dead Kim Young-hoon immediately, grant you all a great enlightenment, and send you on your way unharmed."

"By 'hand over' you mean..."

"Leave it here and disappear."

"...If I give you my subordinate...will you return him?"

"I will decide that. Give me just one Star Shattering stage, and I will bestow upon you an unparalleled blessing and grace. I am also well aware of the nature of you Enders, so I will make every effort to ensure it does not bring misfortune upon you. So...hand it over to me right now."

I glare at Hyeon Mu and answer.

"I refuse. He...is my subordinate and my family."

Absolutely not.

Just like that time...

—Phenomena Extinguishing Mantra!

Just like that time when I lost everything to the Great Mountain Supreme Deity...

I will not easily hand my family over to another.

Kiiiiiiiing!

A tremendous pain surges within my body.

: : I prophesize... If the Northern Heavenly Venerable True Martial Great Emperor Hyeon Mu harms me, Hyeon Mu will face the Heavenly Venerable of the Underworld and inevitably be obstructed by the Judges...! : :

The power of a prophecy depends on how strong the probability of it coming true is.

For example, [the apple hanging from the tree falls to the ground] is an overwhelmingly powerful prophecy.

Because that apple [is bound to fall eventually].

The stronger its natural inevitability, the more powerful the prophecy becomes, reinforcing its absoluteness.

Conversely, [the apple hanging from the tree soars upwards] is a prophecy whose inevitability approaches zero, making its absoluteness weak and causing it to consume an excessive amount of power.

In other words, since it is only natural that the Judges would aid the Heavenly Venerable of the Underworld when Hyeon Mu confronts Them, the absoluteness of my prophecy deepens.

This will be troublesome for Hyeon Mu.

No matter how strong she is, even she would find it difficult to battle the Heavenly Venerable of the Underworld with all the Judges gathered. She would need to fight one-on-one for any chance of victory.

Thus, she would have to lure the Underworld into a place where the Judges are absent...

But with my prophecy in place, such lures or traps would be exceedingly difficult to establish.

However, I add one more thing to it.

: : This is...a prophecy I stake my life upon! : :

Kugugugung!

The pain sharpens further, turning into hooks that latch onto something fundamental within me.

A True Immortal's life-bound prophecy.

And not just any prophecy, but one that reinforces the absoluteness of an already [inevitable] truth.

I see Hyeon Mu's face contorting into that of an evil spirit.

Starting from True Immortality, even the lowest Vestige Liberation Immortal can impose such a 'restriction' upon a Heavenly Venerable by staking their life.

That is why we are able to assert our rights even against celestial beings.

"...You are a bold one. To stake upon yourself the very prophecy I would hate the most... Is that subordinate of yours truly so precious to you?"

"I have already told you. He is my family."

"...Family, you say. Hahaha...! Very well. If that is your resolve...then I, too, shall tell you something."

More precisely, she points at Hong Fan within me.

"I could not read its existence. However, by reading its Qi, I discovered something...A rather unamusing truth."

"Disgust."

"...Pardon?"

"When I sensed its presence, I felt an unbearable disgust. A revulsion that surpassed the feeling of bugs crawling all over my body, a visceral nausea and disgust that made me want to vomit from deep within my chest..."

Hong Fan's presence caused such a feeling in Hyeon Mu?

"Are you trying to kill my subordinate just because of disgust?"

"The disgust I felt when Hae Nyeong exposed our disgrace...it is the same feeling from then. The being you are hiding within your body is, by its very existence, an immense disgrace to the Heavenly Venerables. Let me ask you one thing... That one. Was it born in the Head Realm?"

"...Yes."

"...Of course... The totality of all ancient force... The secret wonder of hidden history. It is because it was born in the Head Realm that it carries this feeling."

"...Now you understand the causality. Hand it over. That one is a disgrace to the Heavenly Venerables. It is one of the traces of ancient force that we deemed as [wisdom that even we ourselves must not know], that we severed from history and sealed away... Even if it is not by my hand, the other Heavenly Venerables except the Underworld will seek to annihilate it by any means the moment they lay eyes on it."

"..."

"You called it your family? How could that be? Do you think such a dangerous entity, tied to the disgrace of the Heavenly Venerables, would truly consider you as family? Between the fingers of the Heavenly Venerable of Void, her menacing gaze gleams.

'This is dangerous...'

"Hand it over. It is fated to die by our hands anyway. It is dangerous to you as well. Your so-called pitiful notion of family means nothing to it. Offer it to me. That is the only path for everyone."

'Hong Fan is the disgrace of the Heavenly Venerables...?'

But I do not want to agree with that statement.

And more than anything, Hyeon Mu's words are wrong.

"This child is not the disgrace of you all. There seems to be a misunderstanding. I hope this misunderstanding can be resolved."

"You claim him to be the disgrace of the Heavenly Venerables...yet this one has met the Sal Tree Heavenly Venerable before!"

Back in the Ancient Force Realm.

At that time, I had no recollection, but I later recalled everything within the Annihilation Flower upon hearing the Sal Tree Heavenly Venerable's cry.

I'm referring to all the moments spent together with the Sal Tree Heavenly Venerable in the Ancient Force Realm.

"That's..."

"Enough talk. Hand it over. There can be a few reasons why only I am feeling discomfort. For example, it could be because I have the sharpest intuition and the most delicate senses among the Four Heavenly Venerables, meaning that this is a discomfort only a being of my level can perceive.

Sarak—

: : Until you hand over my disgrace, you will sail through darkness within horrifying solitude for all of eternity... : :

'Hong Fan...is Hyeon Mu's disgrace?'

One day, if he regains his memories, he will betray me?

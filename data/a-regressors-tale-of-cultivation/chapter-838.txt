Chapter 835: Side Story - Being Ban Ta's Disciple (4)

After Ban Ta's appearance changed,

Makli Hyun-ah truly lived in enormous chaos.

Ever since feminizing, Ban Ta followed all of Makli Hyun-ah's schedules every single day.

Whether he ate, took care of business, or even slept together with Ahn Geum, he came under the blanket with him and made him review what he taught that day.

His understanding of cultivation has been rising quickly...but Makli Hyun-ah feels like he is going crazy.

But there is someone who seems even more likely to go crazy than Makli Hyun-ah.

It is Ahn Geum.

She looks like she is about to lose her mind from Ban Ta's groundbreaking lunacy.

"Even when we eat, when we take care of business, when we wash, even when we sleep, he follows us into the blanket and keeps whisperwhisperwhisperwhisperwhisper-ing... Is that a Sacred Master!? It's an evil spirit!!"

"...D-Don't scold M-Master too much..."

While Ban Ta suppresses another rebellion, Makli Hyun-ah stops Ahn Geum, who is pouring out harsh trash talk at him.

Ahn Geum speaks with a face that says she is furious.

"What kind of nonsense is that? No matter how much he's your master or your parent, does it seem normal to you to follow you into the blanket and whisperwhisperwhisperwhisperwhisperwhisperwhisper in your ear, saying he's making you review what you learned today!?"

"Still, Master is Master..."

"You keep saying nonsense... Hey, wait a second."

Ahn Geum suddenly stares hard into Makli Hyun-ah's eyes and turns serious.

"...Hey. Don't tell me... No, right?"

"..."

"Ah, please... Tell me it's not true..."

Ahn Geum lets out a sigh and looks up at the sky, and Makli Hyun-ah can't help but blush.

Makli Hyun-ah has fallen in love with three people in his life.

His first love was Makli Hyun-ah's older sister, Lee Seo-ah.

Until Lee Seo-ah, in third grade of elementary school, went to a kendo academy and said she is going to practice what she learned, then beat him with an umbrella like she is beating a dog on the hottest day, Makli Hyun-ah truly loved Lee Seo-ah.

His second love was Ji Hwa, the guild master of the Loot Farming Heavenly Lord Guild that he joined while playing games.

He fell in love at first sight when he saw the photo posted on her SNS.

Until Ji Hwa hit him with a truck, he truly loved her.

And his third love...

He only managed to discover it after coming to the Purple Gold Realm.

Makli Hyun-ah, with his face reddened, clears his throat and shakes his head.

"What are you talking about? There's no way I like Master. Chivalry is dead!"

He discovered Ban Ta's new appearance and fell in love.

Seeing Makli Hyun-ah's face and eyes like that, Ahn Geum looks at him with eyes mixed full of emotions like he's pathetic, pitiable, disgusting, miserable, and pitiful.

"What is it that you like so much? Is it the face? Ah, well, the figure is good too, sure."

"I'm telling you it's not! What are you talking about!?"

"You liked the guild master too, didn't you?"

"No! Not at all!"

"If the face is pretty, you fall right away. Hm, no, more than that, the guild master and the Sacred Master have some kind of a similar feel... Is it just your type? Do you like strong women?"

"I'm telling you it's not!!!"

"Thinking about it, Lee Seo-ah had that kind of feel too. Did your older sister influence you and give you that taste? Well, even now you're holding hands with me because you're trying to find your older sister... You really liked your older sister a lot since you were little, didn't you?"

"Don't make me into some weirdo!! I said no!!"

Makli Hyun-ah screams as if he is wronged, and Ahn Geum speaks while picking her ear as if it is noisy.

"Well, since it's come to this, there's nothing we can do. Sacred Master Ban Ta is going to keep following us around, and you also look like you have no intention of stopping it somehow because you like Sacred Master Ban Ta following you around... No matter what I do, it won't mean much. I'll wait for your realm to rise, and during Axis Foundation Practice I'll take you around with me and examine your body."

"Do whatever you want."

Makli Hyun-ah turns his head away from Ahn Geum for no reason, with the feeling that she has found out his secret.

But Ahn Geum grabs his chin, turns it back, and smiles.

"It's not something that works just because I do whatever I want. It's only possible when you quickly cross the Heavenly Being stage and rise to the realm that corresponds to the Four-Axis stage."

"Why are you grabbing my chin...? No, more than that, I also want to reach the level of the Four-Axis stage as soon as possible! But Master says that before I raise my realm to the Four-Axis stage, I must break through not with social authority through people, but by sensing the Plane of Soul myself, converting the power of the soul into authority power, and only then breaking through the realm—so what am I supposed to do!?"

"Hmph, so dull. Do you still not know why your master Sacred Master Ban Ta leaves me, who tried to kidnap you, even though we are engaged, right by your side without placing any constraints or restriction spells on me?"

"Uh, uhh?"

Ahn Geum grabs Makli Hyun-ah's chin, forcefully presses her lips to his, and blows an enormous amount of ghostly energy into him through her mouth.

"Mmph, mmph!"

When an overwhelming amount of ghostly energy is injected all at once, the whites of Makli Hyun-ah's eyes turn black and his pupils turn white, becoming reversed.

At the same time, his lips, fingernails, and toenails also stain black.

"Puha!"

Only after she blows ghostly energy into Makli Hyun-ah up to a limit he absolutely cannot endure does she finally pull her lips away.

Makli Hyun-ah, with his face reddened, rubs his own lips.

"Digest it and convert it into social power. It's going to increase your growth speed."

"Y-You..."

"If you and I stay stuck together, I can blow in ghostly energy that straddles the Plane of Soul and accelerate your growth speed, so that's why he doesn't put any constraints on me. Digest it as soon as possible and convert it into social power. That way your realm rises, I can use cultivation as an excuse to take you around and investigate you, and you can save your older sister too. Isn't that right?"

"..."

Makli Hyun-ah blushes and nods.

"...Okay."

It is a somewhat embarrassing method, but,

In any case, it is something that is necessary in the process of Makli Hyun-ah saving his family.

Thus, from that day on, he moves forward, cooperating with Ahn Geum while continuously converting ghostly energy into social power.

"Uh...pardon...?"

"Wh-What did you say?"

"What do you mean, what? Since the preparations to reach the Four-Axis stage are complete, I'm telling you to go out to other Middle Realms and do Axis Foundation Practice."

On top of that, after the God of Heavenly Punishment died, the Heavenly Tribulation stopped being controlled and has become far stronger than in the mythic age 40 billion years ago. You know that too, right?"

"Yes, I have read about it in the myth records."

"Right. And if there is someone helping, the Heavenly Tribulation itself becomes stronger. And cultivators belonging to Ban Ta Castle are influenced by the altruism inherent in my very existence, and that itself is treated as Immortal Art, so they are unconditionally treated as 'receiving my help.' Meaning inside the Purple Gold Realm, the Heavenly Tribulation unconditionally becomes stronger."

Ban Ta continues while looking at Makli Hyun-ah and Ahn Geum.

"For that reason. Even though you feel uneasy, for my disciple's sake, I permit you to go around to Middle Realms such as Vast Cold, Nether Ghost, Ancient Force, and the True Devil Realm for Axis Foundation Practice."

"...Are you not afraid that I will receive help from a True Immortal and kidnap Hyun-ah again?"

"Hmph, try it. I have already taken measures."

Snap!

When Ban Ta snaps his fingers, Makli Hyun-ah is already moved to the seat beside Ban Ta.

"Uh, what is this...huh!?"

However, Makli Hyun-ah realizes that it is not that he moved to Ban Ta's side, but that only his spirit was dragged straight over to Ban Ta's side in an instant.

"I have already planted my mark. If those other True Immortals try to kidnap my disciple recklessly, I made it so I can immediately summon my disciple's soul to the Purple Gold Realm. With the authority of the Purple Gold Realm Sacred Master, I can increase something like the number of revivals of a Four-Axis stage cultivator to infinity, so whenever my disciple suffers a major mishap, I can just summon only the soul and revive him in the Purple Gold Realm."

"Thorough."

"It concerns my disciple, so of course. And through this, every night when Hyun-ah falls asleep, I will summon my disciple's soul and give supplemental lessons, such as cultivation guidance."

"Heheok!"

Makli Hyun-ah goes blank.

Before, he could at least sleep, but from now on he won't even be able to sleep.

Still, Ahn Geum does not seem to care and nods.

"Well, do as you please on that point. As long as you guarantee time alone for the two of us, whatever."

"Good. Then start with raising your realm right away, and prepare to move to other Middle Realms immediately!"

"Y-Yes? Pardon?"

Makli Hyun-ah blinks at the sudden change, but nothing changes.

After a while, he boards Ahn Geum's Nether Crossing Ship and moves to the Mind Domain among the eight regions of the Purple Gold Realm, a place from which he can move to other Middle Realms.

Kugugugu!

"You don't have to follow along."

"I am also going as Hyun-ah's guardian and your monitor. Inside my body, a single strand of the Sacred Master's split soul resides. In a distant dimension where the Purple Gold Realm's principle does not reach, if you try to kidnap Hyun-ah to another Heavenly Domain, the Sacred Master will immediately descend into me and punish you."

"That's scary. Sorry, but that is not going to happen, so please don't just interfere with my time with Hyun-ah."

"As long as you don't do anything suspicious."

Makli Cheon-sa, who follows along as Makli Hyun-ah's guardian and Ahn Geum's monitor, sits on the Nether Crossing Ship and prepares to help Makli Hyun-ah with his Heavenly Tribulation that is about to arrive.

Wo-woong!

In one part of the Mind Domain, where sacred flower pollen blooms in full and makes it look like a Peach Garden Paradise, a dimensional gate to another Middle Realm opens.

Ahn Geum pilots the Nether Crossing Ship and flies into the dimensional gate with the others, then speaks to Makli Hyun-ah.

"Start the Four-Axis stage advancement the moment we leave the Purple Gold Realm. If you advance after arriving in another Middle Realm for no reason, the Heavenly Tribulation could get weirdly tangled because of other energies."

"Hmph, I know too, so don't worry."

Makli Hyun-ah sits in a lotus position and prepares to advance, and Ahn Geum watches him for a moment, then quickly controls the Nether Crossing Ship and goes out beyond the Purple Gold Realm's boundary.

And in the next moment,

"We got engaged."

Lee Seo-ah stares at the two with a stupid look, and Ahn Geum covers Makli Hyun-ah's mouth as if to block what he is about to say, then speaks to Lee Seo-ah.

"...You...you bitch..."

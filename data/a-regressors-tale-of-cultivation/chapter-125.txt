Therefore, each glass sword emits different colors when interconnected due to these subtle differences.

And the range of changes that these temperaments undergo within the storm of three thousand glass swords is,

Infinite!

They echo the limitless freedom of the Formless Sword!

One temperament, when in a different position, becomes a completely different tool.

A single glass sword continuously changes its position according to my manipulation and strikes relentlessly.

This continuously changing glass sword is three thousand in number.

[Argh, this bastard...!]

Yuan Li unleashes a blood wind with his blood-colored leaf fan, blocking the glass swords.

But I manipulate the glass swords even more precisely.

Infinite change is in my hands.

The Formless Sword only gains freedom of trajectory, not of temperament.

However, by obtaining the Colorless Glass Swords, the Formless Sword reaches a more complete realm of freedom.

Chalalalak!

Sharp temperament, soft temperament, a moderate temperament between the two, hard temperament, flowing temperament...

The energy of those innumerable temperaments all move according to my will, dying the surroundings in a brilliance of colors.

Those changes devour Yuan li’s blood storm, gradually swallowing him up.

[Hmph, don't make me laugh!]

Yuan Li scoffs, infusing energy into his dagger dharma treasures.

The seventeen bone daggers rotate crazily around him, scattering bright red streaks.

The bright red streaks seem to completely engulf him.

But I just smile.

It’s laughable.

Shoong!

Among the numerous changes, my vision accurately finds a gap.

No matter how tightly the streaks surround him, there is always enough room to penetrate.

The Colorful Glass Swords rush towards him.

Severing Mountain Swordsmanship, Layered Mountains, Qi Mountain Heart Sky!

Swish!

Yuan Li regenerates his body in the given time.

[Good, quite impressive.]

[To push me this far, I give my respects. I too will properly fight now.]

Squirm.

My brows twitch upon seeing it.

Crack…

I grind my teeth, veins bulging on my forehead.

I recognize that familiar aura.

It’s Kim Young-hoon's Inner Core.

His remains, not found in Kim Young-hoon's body, were unsurprisingly taken by Yuan Li.

Whirr!

The incantations glow red, emitting a strange attraction.

The bizarre ghost beast.

The two blood cloud ghost kings.

The three ghosts are drawn to the Inner Core’s attraction, beginning to merge around it.

Rumble!

Pop, pop, pop!

Seventeen bone dagger dharma treasures..

Yuan Li embeds sixteen of these daggers into various parts of the Asura.

As for the last remaining dagger,

Yuan Li cut off three of his fingers, feeding them one by one into the Asura's mouth.

Yuan Li's self-amputated fingers do not regenerate, and the Asura's cultivation surges even more after consuming them.

Kugugugu!

Core Formation Grand Perfection!

The Asura's power reaches just short of Nascent Soul.

[With his Demon Core, I’ve created this excellent result. You, his friend, have learned a similar strange method despite appearing different. You must have a similar Demon Core, right? I’ll extract it and make good use of yours too…!]

Yuan Li manipulates the Asura from behind, shouting.

[Bring it on!]

"Colorless Glass Sword, Second Form. Natural."

I release the second form of the Colorless Glass Sword while looking at him.

The Colorless Glass Sword, Second Form, Natural, has a simple effect.

The Colorless Glass Swords become more strongly interconnected, creating a perfect link of energy.

The three thousand Glass Swords, in their connected state, begin to circulate energy among themselves.

This circulation seamlessly incorporates my Formless Sword, allowing it to flow in unison with them

Thus, they achieve a more profound interconnectedness, facilitating a unified energy circulation.

That is the second form of the Colorless Glass Sword, Natural.

The greatest advantage of "Natural" is, of course, the ability to instantly circulate the Formless Sword to the desired location in accordance with the circulating energy, concentrating or dispersing the power of the Formless Sword.

The Colorless Glass Swords line up in a row.

The Formless Sword housed within the glass swords focus its energy at the tip of the lined-up swords due to the circulation of "Natural."

Clang!

Driven by sword control, the glass swords pierce directly into the Asura.

It's uncertain whether the Asura feels pain or not, as it shows no expression while gripping and swinging its spear.

However, the glass swords quickly disperse and reposition themselves, and once again under my control, they begin flying around the Asura, inflicting wounds on its body.

Numerous changes occur.

The Asura swings the blood-colored leaf fan on one side, the blood-colored spear on another, and the crystal skull staff on yet another, scattering various spells.

Explosions resonate, distorting the area in all directions.

But I continue on, controlling the storm of glass to pressurize the Asura.

"Colorless Glass Sword. First Form. Second Form. Combine."

And thus, the true strength of the Colorless Glass Sword begins to emerge.

First Form, Color.

Second Form, Natural.

When both forms are activated simultaneously,

The terror of the Colorless Glass Sword is fully revealed.

The interconnected Glass Swords activate their spiritual energy circuits, shining in various colors.

Then, in that state, the energy of the Glass Swords begin to circulate.

Flash!

A sword that once emitted black turns white.

The Colorless Glass Swords, each originally having their unique hue, begin to continuously share and change colors without pause.

A cascade of colors dance, forming a luminous tempest that envelopes the Asura.

The infinite trajectory of the Formless Sword merges with the endless shifts of temperaments of the Colorless Glass Sword.

Together, they display an array of transformations beyond words.

The blood-colored Asura is but a small moth trapped in a storm.

Gradually, more wounds appear on the surface of the Asura.

The storm of glass seems wildly chaotic, but each sword follows the group of swords.

Sizzle!

The wounds on the Asura's body multiplies, soon covering it entirely.

Severing Mountain Swordsmanship.

Twenty-Second Move.

Ultimate Technique.

As the Colorless Glass Swords unleash Severing Mountain, the Formless Sword housed within them amplifies the transformative power in Severing Mountain, grinding the Asura from all angles.

Flash!

A burst of light erupts, and the Asura explodes completely.

I rush forward, snatching Kim Young-hoon's Inner Core from the center of the Asura, then control the Colorless Glass Swords.

"Disperse!"

Swish!

The Glass Swords scatter in all directions, clearing a path, and at the end of the path stands Yuan Li.

"Fire!"

At that moment, the Nether Crossing Ship's main cannon, which had been gathering energy, fires.

Yuan Li tries to dodge, but I form a seal.

Kududuk!

While distracted, I had stealthily placed a cluster of curses behind him.

It isn't just me.

Cheongmun Jung-jin shouts.

"Byeok Clan head, now! Use it now!"

Byeok Cheon-gi also throws the talisman infused with spiritual power towards Yuan Li.

The talisman emits light as it flies towards Yuan Li.

Yuan Li, reduced to a mere early Core Formation stage, is hit squarely by the main cannon of the Nether Crossing Ship.

Flash!!!

Light erupts in all directions.

We all stare at the center of the explosion.

Manli Min-lap, too, focuses on the center without making any remarks like ‘We've killed him this time.’

And then.

No more spiritual pressure can be felt from Yuan Li.

Not a single trace of spiritual energy.

The color of their intent is odd.

And Byeok Cheon-gi stands beside Yuan Li with a triumphant expression.

"B-Bye-Byeok Clan head...!!!?"

Cheongmun Jung-jin’s eyes, seeing one of the three most important people in the war against Yuan Li, Byeok Cheon-gi, standing beside Yuan Li, bulge with rage.

Swish!

Outside the Serving Command Palace.

From Yuan Li's black castle, the same crimson river that had come earlier enters through the gates of Serving Command Palace.

Swish!

As Yuan Li absorbs the crimson river, his spiritual power begins to rise again. Seemingly displeased, he moves his hand.

[To make me use so much of my precious Long Source True Power…. The Long Source True Power I've gathered for hundreds of years…. Well, it doesn't matter. There won't be many chances to extract so much life force from Core Formation cultivators like today.]

"Haha, Sir Bloodwood. There's only about a moment left until Serving Command Palace’s restraint releases."

"Please show us the defensive and offensive power of the Heavenly Being stage."

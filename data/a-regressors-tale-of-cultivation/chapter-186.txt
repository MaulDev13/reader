Author's Note:

A minor setting collection that does not have a significant impact on the plot.

The relationships between the Immortal Beasts appearing in the Regressor’s Tale:

The Black Dragon and the Supreme Tiger do not get along and fight whenever they meet. These two beasts generally do not get along with most other beasts and growl whenever they meet.

The Azure Peng tries to eat the offspring of the Black Dragon upon meeting and flees from the Supreme Tiger due to being overpowered. It gets along with all other Immortal Beasts except for the Lustrous Glaze Peacock.

The Azure Peng tries to kill the Lustrous Glaze Peacock upon meeting, while the Lustrous Glaze Peacock tries to lay its eggs when meeting the Azure Peng. Not just with the Azure Peng, but it also desires to mate with the Supreme Tiger, the Black Dragon, the Mountain-Destroying Demon Ape, and the White Winged Heavenly Pegasus, making it the most ill-reputed among the beasts. However, it avoids the Yin Ghost Black Turtle as its yin energy does not benefit its eggs.

The Yin Ghost Black Turtle, when meeting the Mountain-Destroying Demon Ape, brings ghosts to have a joyous time together. It is a welcomed figure among Immortal Beasts with strong yin energy but is shunned by those with strong yang energy.

The Mountain-Destroying Demon Ape and the White Winged Heavenly Pegasus, due to the nature of the powers they possess, inevitably cause natural disasters when they meet, regardless of whether their relationship is good or bad. These two Immortal Beasts always maintain a neutral relationship with other Immortal Beasts.

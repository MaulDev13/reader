"What lesson shall I give today?"

Glass Peacock still has not caught me.

However, my comrades also have not yet sent a signal that they escaped from the Bearing Tree Heavenly Domain.

But without a doubt, they have read my Qi and discerned the actions I have taken.

Even if they do not know the secret of my regression, they have to some extent read the fixed flow of my history caused by it.

They should only be able to practice cultivation using Immortal Arts and only freely wield prophecy after reaching Heaven Upper Immortal. However...strangely enough, all Vestige Liberation Immortals use prophecy."

"Even those who rose through the Plane of Qi, not the Plane of Fate, those who were Earth Tribe cultivators during their Entering Nirvana stage, are still able to use prophecy. Even if they are entities who transcend planes...isn't that strange? Therefore, the sages of the Immortal Realm have proposed these hypotheses.

"The first hypothesis: fate and history are actually one and the same. While it may seem like only fate can be prophesied, in reality, history can also be touched upon—we just do not know the method.

"The second hypothesis: Fate is the greatest law of the heavens, and so its power surpasses all other absolute laws. That is why prophecy is possible.

"The third hypothesis: Among the existing Supreme Deities, [someone] has obtained the authority over Fate (命運), or at the very least, the authority closest to it. This is why Vestige Liberation Immortals find it [easier] to use prophecy...or so they claim.""

"Hrmm... You really are quite the knowledge-hungry one."

Glass Peacock pushes further into my Filling the Heavens Governing View, brushing through its mist.

Once I regain my composure, Glass Peacock's attempts at further intrusion once again end in failure.

"Of course, I have a different perspective. That is why I am forming a fourth hypothesis. Are you curious? If you are, then come within my arms. I shall whisper it to you within my embrace."

"..."

"Hehe, such a feisty one. I will embrace you, no matter what."

Glass Peacock licks their lips before continuing their explanation.

"All three hypotheses have flaws. The first hypothesis—fate and history are one? That doesn't hold up. Heaven Immortals and Immortal Beasts, Radiance Hall and the Underworld. The mere fact that these irreconcilable opposites exist disproves it. Fate is [convergence], while history is [diffusion]. The two can never be the same. They are fundamentally opposing forces."

Slowly, they reveal shocking truths as they come closer to me.

"The second hypothesis—fate is the greatest? That's impossible. If that were the case, Radiance Hall would have long since crushed the Underworld. In the Underworld, the number of Heaven Immortals who cultivate prophecy is so small they can be counted within ten fingers. And the Heavenly Venerables! Even though the Heavenly Venerables, as Heaven Immortals, rose to their positions to pursue the power of history, they still devote themselves to cultivating in the authority of historical observation. The only one who rose to Heavenly Venerable purely from the Earth Tribe or as an Earth Immortal is the Underworld."

I calm my heart and listen to Glass Peacock's words.

"And the last one. Honestly, it is the most nonsensical of them all. Fate, history, and what...the first light? Those three laws are, quite literally, [absolute]."

"..."

"They are in a completely different dimension from the typical Immortal Dao, Seat, or Origin Essence, which require an imperfect interpretation. Fate, history, and the third law are absolutely incapable of possessing a Seat."

I raise a question in response to their words.

"Is it not possible for there to be a Seat?"

"Haha! Absolutely not. That is impossible. It seems you don't understand what a Seat truly is."

Glass Peacock, excited by my question, pushes further into Filling the Heavens Governing View and continues their lecture.

"If I were to explain as someone who has once achieved a Seat myself...a Seat is like a mountain. Reaching the summit of the mountain can be said to be akin to seizing a Seat. It is, in a way, an apex."

Listening to Glass Peacock's explanation of Seats and the Immortal Dao, I steadily accumulate wisdom.

And so, 300 years pass once more.

"The Immortal Dao is like the path up a mountain. The Immortal Dao that descends from a Seat holds countless paths, and how one reaches the peak of the mountain is what it means to 'interpret the Seat.' Just as one can climb from the south, but also from the north, a Seat can be interpreted in reverse. So long as one pushes through with that interpretation, they will eventually reach the peak."

Before I realize it, only about thirty zhang remain between Glass Peacock and me.

Perhaps in a hundred years, only a barrier as thin as glass will separate us.

Kwa-ching!

Glass Peacock shatters another barrier and takes another step closer to me.

"Your comrades still have not escaped from the spatial labyrinth that this Immortal has cast. Give up. I will embrace you warmly."

"Good... That heart, I hope it remains unshaken even after enduring three million years with me. In any case, have you finally grasped why a Seat cannot exist for the Three Great Laws?"

Because the Earth is round, and there is no such thing as a summit.

The sun, the moon, the Earth—celestial bodies!

Since they have no end, one cannot reach their apex—such are the three absolute laws!

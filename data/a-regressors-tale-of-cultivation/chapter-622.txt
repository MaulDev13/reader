I recall the contents of the Upper Immortal cultivation and smile bitterly.

The formula of the Upper Immortal cultivation consists of two methods: Yang Pitch (陽律) and Yin Pitch (陰呂), and one can cultivate by following either method.

The formula of Yang Pitch is simple.

It involves either flourishing six different races and civilizations within a single Heavenly Domain, or flourishing a single race across six different Heavenly Domains.

However, the problem lies with Yin Pitch.

Yin Pitch entails erasing six different civilizations and races within a single Heavenly Domain entirely, or erasing one civilization and race from six different Heavenly Domains.

And in the case of Yang Pitch, there is an additional condition: the race and civilization must be [races and civilizations that have never existed before].

A malicious way of cultivation designed to be easier to advance one's realm through destruction alone.

That is the formula of the Upper Immortal cultivation—Yang Pitch and Yin Pitch.

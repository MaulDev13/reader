Chapter 594: Mount Sumeru (7)

"Dual...dual cultivation?"

I am left speechless for a moment and stare at Jeon Myeong-hoon.

He also seems dumbfounded, looking at me with vacant eyes.

He doesn't know much about the Heavenly Venerable of the Void, but at least he seems to understand that they are some kind of incredible existence.

Yet, such an existence suddenly brings up something like dual cultivation out of nowhere, leaving even him bewildered.

'What in the world is this person thinking?'

I try to read Hyeon Mu's intent, but as expected, not a single thought leaks out, likely because they are a being who has transcended Severing Heaven.

Nothing is reflected.

How long do I contemplate Hyeon Mu's intentions?

"...Hm, I apologize, but I believe that dual cultivation should be done between those who share a mutual sentiment."

"Mm?"

Hyeon Mu looks at me as if she does not understand.

"That is strange. Do you not have feelings for me?"

Seeing her smile meaningfully, I feel a headache coming on.

"Yes...! I do have feelings. But...that feeling is not one of wanting to dual cultivate. It is the heart of a martial artist who wishes to challenge the limits of Martial Arts."

"Martial artist? So, you are saying that dual cultivation with me is not an act of testing the realm of Martial Arts?"

"Yes? I mean...hmm...I do not understand how it can be interpreted that way."

"Are you not seeking guidance from Kim Young-hoon?"

"In any case, if you two wish to meet him, one of you must inevitably dual cultivate with me. But that fool over there would never survive my intense dual cultivation, so...Seo Eun-hyun, you must be the one to do it with me."

A headache creeps up as I question her.

"...No, I do not understand why dual cultivation with the Heavenly Venerable is necessary to meet Kim Young-hoon. If he is connected to a spacetime of emptiness or within the domain of purity, could you not simply guide us there through a technique that immerses us into the domain of purity?"

"That is also true... However, his current location is a rather special one. The technique you use to reach the domain of purity..."

"...So, you are saying that dual cultivation is necessary to access that specific spacetime."

"That is correct. And only someone at your level can endure it, which is why I am making the offer to you."

"Kkuum..."

Hyeon Mu's expression is serious.

She does not seem to be joking.

'There would be no reason for an existence of her level to lie to me or make fun of me...But dual cultivation? Dual cultivation, of all things...'

It's when I spend some time in thought.

Hyeon Mu clicks her tongue and speaks.

"You seem to have many thoughts. Could it be that you lack confidence in dual cultivation?"

"Pardon? N-No, that is not the case, but..."

"Enough. If you lack confidence, just watch from the sidelines and gain enlightenment. I will do it with this one instead..."

Hyeon Mu approaches Jeon Myeong-hoon, and I, dumbfounded, watch the two.

Then, it happens.

Jeon Myeong-hoon pulls Jin So-hae's hand from his embrace and shouts.

"Gal (喝)! I do not know which noble being you are, but! The only person in this world I will ever dual cultivate with is only one, my true love...So-hae! Please, do not trample on my heart!"

"..."

At those words, the Heavenly Venerable of Void looks at Jeon Myeong-hoon and begins reading his intent and lingering thoughts.

And after a short while...

Hyeon Mu presses her temple with her index finger as if she has a headache.

"...Ah, I see. I was wondering why we were not understanding each other...The meaning of the word was different. Damn it. Because you have completely sealed your intent, I could not interpret your meaning properly."

"P-Pardon?"

For some reason, Hyeon Mu looks at me with somewhat disdainful eyes and speaks.

"Just what do you think dual cultivation is?"

"P-Pardon? Well... Mixing yin and yang, is it not something like that?"

At my response, Hyeon Mu clicks her tongue and speaks.

"...Tsk. What a filthy-minded fool. Did you only ever think of dual cultivation in that way?"

"N-No..."

"Dual cultivation (雙修) refers to any cultivation method where two or more cultivators give and receive what they need to improve. Bedroom arts and Yin-Yang unity techniques are just one aspect of it. Well, I suppose a young one like you, who has only recently become True Immortal, would still be ignorant and have shallow knowledge..."

"..."

"The dual cultivation I am referring to is..."

"...I see. I apologize for showing such vulgar behavior..."

"I heard your Immortal Title is Crystal Glass Being. Are you trying to become a disciple of Glass Peacock or something?"

"..."

"...Enough. So, will you do it or not? If you dual cultivate with me, you will be able to reach Kim Young-hoon's location in approximately 100 years."

"...I will do it."

Since it is that kind of dual cultivation, there is no reason to refuse.

"Good. Then follow me. From now on...let us find a suitable place to dual cultivate."

Jeon Myeong-hoon and I follow Hyeon Mu as we move towards the inner core of a suitable planet and settle there.

Thus begins my dual cultivation with Hyeon Mu, spanning nearly one hundred years.

Beneath the surface of a planet in the Good Sight Heavenly Domain.

"Haaa..."

"Do you enjoy it? Dual cultivation?"

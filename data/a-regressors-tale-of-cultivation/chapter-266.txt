Thus, despite using the Extinguishing Divine Tribulating Heavens Technique, the main force of the Golden Divine Heavenly Thunder Sect, so to speak, had mostly died.

However, those not considered the main force,

Those whom even the Heavenly Tribulation seemed to show little interest in.

The Qi Refining disciples, or those who were not inside Thunder Spirit Island at that point, have not yet died.

'20 years...All disciples of the Golden Divine Heavenly Thunder Sect are fated to die within the next 20 years due to the Heavenly Tribulation.'

In my past life, even though it was said to be 20 years, they all perished within a few months due to the Heavenly Tribulation.

Extinguishing Divine Tribulating Heavens had succeeded in delaying the celestial energy!

'There's at least a year or two's worth of' time.'

The golden lightning I had captured will continue to cry out to kill the remaining disciples of the Golden Divine Heavenly Thunder Sect in the plane of Fate.

A treacherous world where even a distinguished being who threatened the Owner of Heavenly Punishment, a world where even their friend, had died.

"There exists a place where even the Owner of Heavenly Punishment cannot follow."

The Head Realm.

Kugugugugu!

Kang Min-hee is chasing from behind, while a snake is flickering its tongue beside me, making a suspicious proposal.

I know this bastard well.

No matter what the situation, a liar who only utters empty words.

Information about Seo Hweol's main body.

"But there are conditions."

"According to your words, we need to work together to snap the Mad Lord back to his senses, right? During that process, you will stay out of my sight and act only remotely."

If that bastard were to meet my comrades and brainwash them with his filthy Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens, I would be extremely furious.

What if my comrades turn into copies of Seo Hweol?

Grit—

Just imagining it makes my teeth grind.

I can't afford to lose any more comrades carelessly.

If such a thing happens, I might lose control of my anger and lose my reason, leading me to do something even I can't predict.

'Maybe I might even lose my mind and start chanting the Phenomena Extinguishing Mantra until the Owner of Great Mountain descends.'

[If it's necessary, I will comply. Don't worry. Until I find my main body, I have no choice but to rely on my madam and Daoist Seo.]

"..."

A highly suspicious and dubious alliance.

But in the current situation, it's necessary.

"Fine. In that case...I will form a temporary alliance with you."

[Hoho. Thank you for your kindness, Daoist Seo...]

He smiles widely and bows as if he is genuinely pleased.

"...In any case..."

[Aaaaaahhh—]

"It seems we'll need to deal with the Ghost Guiding Holy Mother first before we can reach the Mad Lord. Any ideas?"

To expel Kang Min-hee from the realm, we need the Mad Lord's help.

But to meet and persuade the Mad Lord, I, Seo Hweol, Kim Yeon, and Oh Hye-seo will first have to separate from Kang Min-hee before combining our strengths.

However, Kang Min-hee is chasing me like crazy.

If this continues, we won't even be able to start the alliance, let alone anything else.

Especially for a powerful ghostly being like her, my death aura would be extremely conspicuous.

'Unless I escape from the Star Shattering stage sight in one go, she will endlessly chase after me.'

Since things have come to this, I decide to test Seo Hweol's cunning and ask him for a strategy.

[There is a plan that will buy you enough time to meet up with your comrades. Would you like to hear it?]

"Speak quickly."

[Head to Heaven-Earth Palace.]

[Go to Heaven-Earth Palace and lure that thing to the Flying Immortal Platform—in other words, drop it under the Void Spirit Pond. Since it seems to lack intelligence, it won't be too difficult. [Isn't that rather a good thing? I understand you don't have a good relationship with the Heaven-Earth Palace Lord Hon Won.]

"Stop wagging your sly tongue and help me out. With your skills and ambition, it's hard to believe you haven't already placed Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens on the living beings residing in the Heaven-Earth central region. Use your ability to evacuate everyone there right now."

[As expected, I can't outmatch Daoist Seo. But evacuating 'everyone' is impossible.]

The [name] buried within Hon Won, Cheon Ra!

I hoped to shove such a dangerous task to Seo Hweol, but it seems I will have to face it myself.

From the character 監 (supervision) in his eye, to the Great Mountain Splitting Emperor Technique he has learned.

To find out about Hon Won's current state,

Swooooosh—

The same purple dragon robe, with hair sticking out messily from beneath his manggeon, and his disheveled topknot—a worn and haggard appearance.

Suuuk—

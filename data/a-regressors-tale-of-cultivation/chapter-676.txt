Chapter 672: Holy Mother (2)

"A baby... Why, why something like that...?"

Yes, this is a matter of necessity.

Because Oh Hyun-seok wants it.

And also because Kim Young-hoon's True Attainment envisions a future where all of us reach the Audience Chamber.

If we want to grant Oh Hyun-seok's wish, and if we want to meet Kim Young-hoon again, we must save Oh Hye-seo.

Then it happens.

"...!"

Tssaaaah!

The chaos of the Outer Sea separates and order is born.

The planes of Qi, Fate, and Soul emerge, and above Glass Peacock's hand, a minimum space where living beings can dwell is created.

Inside that tiny, droplet-sized space, it looks like a miniature universe.

"As you know, all beings possess Qi. The same goes for Fate. Their origin is unknowable, and they can only be said to be this world itself. Therefore, although all laws in the world have Origin Essences, only the two absolute laws—Qi and Fate—are without."

"..."

"But...do you know this? The true secret of the Plane of Soul."

"...I do. The domain of the Northern Heavenly Venerable True Martial Great Emperor. The Interdimensional Void is actually the reverse side of the Plane of Soul."

"Yes, that's right. Then let me ask you one thing."

At Glass Peacock's next question, I let out a low hum.

"Then in this world, are 'souls' something created by the Heavenly Venerable of Void?"

"..."

Souls.

Spirits.

That which we call by those names...

They certainly exist in this world.

"...I don't know."

But I mutter quietly in thought at the topic Glass Peacock has thrown.

Where do our souls come from?

What is the origin of our spirits?

"What's certain is, it is not the Plane of Soul. That place is merely where souls linger..."

"Right. The spirit is truly bizarre. Something like Heaven and Earth spiritual energy is produced once one reaches the Star Shattering stage. Fate is produced upon becoming a Great Net Immortal. But spirit? It just suddenly appears all at once one day, then disappears after passing through reincarnation. And though you can crush the spirit or absorb spiritual energy from it, no matter what you do, you can't completely annihilate the soul itself. Of course, the reason souls can't be destroyed is mostly because of the power of the Underworld… Regardless, the spirit cannot be 'artificially' produced by anyone."

Indeed, with Qi or Fate, once you reach a certain realm, you can create them at will.

But with Soul, no matter what methods are used, it cannot be created artificially.

Therefore, when True Immortals perform Yang Pitch cultivation and create races, even if they may manipulate the bodies or fate of the race with their own hands, they still acquire the spirit through the Underworld and the Eastern Heaven Flower Field.

"The spirit is thus extremely unstable. Its cycle of creation is unclear, and we don't even know under what conditions it arises. But among the older True Immortals, a few know one method by which the spirit is created."

"What...is it?"

"Emotion."

"Emotion...?"

"That's right. What is called the heart of an existence. Among those, when positive emotions like 'joy (喜)', 'love (愛)', or 'pleasure (樂)' reach their peak...that is when the spirit is created. Occasionally, there are beings born into this world by chance who were never granted life by the Eastern Heaven Flower Field. Such beings are usually children whose parents were not originally granted a child, but their love and joy became so intense that it surpassed fate, causing spirit to be born from them."

"...!"

"Of course...creating a spirit through mere love is not easy. After all, the positive emotion must surpass the power of fate, even if just for a moment, for a spirit to be created. But if the creation succeeds, that spirit exists in a pure state that has never undergone reincarnation and is incredibly clean. In other words...it becomes a spirit full of positive emotions."

Glass Peacock smiles faintly and continues.

"In fact, when I laid an egg in Azure Peng, I saw a spirit being born in Azure Peng's womb with my own eyes. That spirit...was truly a clean spirit. Others don't have such a strong surge in emotional power when they feel joy, but that child...they possessed such a positive heart that when they felt joy, the intent of that joy was strong enough to cover a thousand li in diameter. In other words..."

They point toward Oh Hye-seo's sealing sphere.

"A 'pure spirit' that has not undergone reincarnation greatly amplifies emotion by its very nature. Especially positive emotions. To break through that Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens and the unknown Immortal Art within the Great Mountain Holy Mother, the best method is for you two to copulate and create a spirit, then use the 'emotion-amplifying power' of that pure spirit to melt away Oh Hye-seo's fear and negative emotions."

"...But even if we do create a spirit...how do we know whether that spirit would want to be created?"

"...What?"

"Oh Hye-seo definitely needs to be healed. But that doesn't mean it's okay to create a child just to use them as a tool to heal her! I can not accept creating or producing my child to use as a tool! That child would never have wanted to be created just to serve as a tool either!"

But at my words, Glass Peacock scoffs and replies.

"You're talking nonsense. Don't delude yourself. That's not something for you to decide!"

"...What?"

"To be born is, in itself, an event of immeasurable value. No matter how hellish or painful life is, the very act of living has value! Born as a tool, you say? You think I'm not the same? Being bought right after being born because one of those Heaven Tribe freaks with deviant lusts wanted to use a peacock as a masturbation tool is me! So does that mean the meaning of my life is nothing more than a tool for self-pleasure?"

"...That's..."

"Life can be born as a tool, as a sex object, even as someone's meal... But still, good things exist. If you grit your teeth and keep going, there will come a day when you find your value. Don't you dare judge the value of someone's birth with that petty little head of yours!"

They are right.

No matter how difficult it is, life certainly has value.

Being born has value.

However, one thing is clear.

"You are right. But that still doesn't mean it's okay to have a child to use as a tool."

"The problem lies in your perception of them as a tool. Isn't your child capable of saving the Great Mountain Holy Mother? They are a means of healing her!"

"...Well, I don't love Oh Hye-seo, so that doesn't really resonate with me.

"You..."

At that moment.

Kim Yeon tugs on my back.

"Hm? What is it, Yeon-ah?"

"..."

She silently stares at me, then points to the sealing sphere holding Oh Hye-seo.

—Please, open it for a moment.

"You need to examine something?"

Reading her meaning through Indra's Net, I temporarily release the seal on Oh Hye-seo's body.

Due to Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens sealing her consciousness, Oh Hye-seo remains lying still with her eyes closed even after her body exits the sphere.

Sararak—

Kim Yeon's consciousness thread enters Oh Hye-seo's mind.

Then a short moment later—

Flash!

Kim Yeon's consciousness seems to connect directly into Oh Hye-seo's spirit, and a gleam of light pink blazes in eyes.

Huarurururuk!

"...! Y-Yeon-ah? Kim Yeon! What's happening!"

Kigigigigigik—

"...!"

That imprint seems to blaze in soft pink light, then after slowly detaching from Kim Yeon's body, flutters its wings as if it's alive.

Then.

"...I finally...understand. Thank you, Glass Peacock."

"...!"

I realize what has happened to Kim Yeon.

'What? Did she gain some enlightenment from what Glass Peacock said?'

Kim Yeon has just obtained enlightenment from Glass Peacock and taken one step closer to awakening as a Heavenly King.

'Th-This feeling...'

It's incredibly similar to the power I once felt from Kim Young-hoon.

She is now closely approaching the Tridacna Vast Cold Heavenly King.

"You said Oh Hye-seo is carrying life, didn't you, Eun-hyun Orabeoni?"

"...I did."

Normally, she would have to fully draw out her power to its utmost limits to break those constraints, but it seems the current Kim Yeon can now free herself from them just by thinking.

"Now I finally understand. Only after entering Oh Hye-seo's heart again and confirming it did I realize. The life inside Oh Hye-seo...it has the feeling of a 'created soul,' just like what Glass Peacock spoke of."

"...!"

"And...what I just confirmed is the identity of the being who breathed that soul into Oh Hye-seo."

Huarurururuk—

Her eyes flare blaze in a light pink hue, and she tells me the truth.

"The Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens that Seo Hweol passed on to Oh Hye-seo. It is undoubtedly formed from endless hatred and yoke, but...somewhere deep within it, there is undoubtedly a very small amount of love. And that power...it breathed a spirit into Oh Hye-seo and is currently creating. It's certainly a faint and unstable spirit, but just as Glass Peacock said, it's a 'pure spirit'."

"Maybe...that spirit is the child of Oh Hye-seo and Seo Hweol..."

"...No. That's not it."

"Pardon...?"

'Seo Hweol's heart toward Oh Hye-seo...created a soul. And that means...'

Oh Hye-seo is in a state where she now possesses a soul.

That soul likely isn't a child between her and Seo Hweol, but rather Oh Hye-seo's own soul.

Built upon Seo Hweol's heart, she has partially escaped the bridle of an Ender.

The Brahma Nature of Creation: joy, love, pleasure.

The Narayana Nature of Preservation: anger, sorrow, pleasure.

The Mahesvara Nature of Destruction: hatred, desire, pleasure.

"We are not going to have a child together."

"She can do it. Because Kim Yeon...is a girl with a lot of love."

The Heavenly King who governs love.

The Ender connected to the Triple Divinity of Creation.

The successor of the Tridacna Vast Cold Heavenly King, and my love.

We may be able to awaken a new existence—not just within Oh Hye-seo, but within all of us.

Having a child with Kim Yeon comes after we escape the Outer Sea.

There's no need to create a baby's spirit just to use it as a tool to save her.

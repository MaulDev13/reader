Everything can be absorbed into the Head Realm.

The Vast Cold Heavenly Lord, who is said to have altered the conditions for the End of an entire Heavenly Domain.

If Seo Hweol accelerates the End of the world with attraction force, it will be solely Seo Hweol's feat!

And the Head Realm, having absorbed the world stained with Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens, will ultimately...consume all of Seo Hweol's Tainted Soul Filling the Heavens and will complete Seo Hweol's plan.

Meet the [truth] he desires.

The [gazes] visible within that mist.

Because he had no reason to go mad.

Filling the Heavens Governing View!

My own failure.

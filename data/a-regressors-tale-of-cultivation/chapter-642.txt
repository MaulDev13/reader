That the Heart Tribe are like beings who never originally existed, that barged into the world.

It turns out that was the truth.

The cultivation of the Heart Tribe was never something that truly existed.

Because the system of cultivation that the entity known as the Northern Heavenly Venerable forcefully wedged into this world is the Manifestation of the Heart Tribe.

Why is it that, through Void Perception, which connects to the Interdimensional Void, the talent of the Heart Tribe increases?

Because the Plane of Soul and the Interdimensional Void are two faces of the same thing!

Kim Young-hoon's talent is one intimately tied to the very Plane of Soul.

And now, Kim Young-hoon and I, who have continued our cultivation by borrowing immense power from the Plane of Soul, must pay a horrifying price to Hyeon Mu.

As I feel my energy being sucked out by the overwhelming suction force radiating from Hyeon Mu, her words ring in my ears.

[Repay it. And then dance with me. Please endure... Endure and endure, and while dancing with me...come kill me...]

Thus, realizing Hyeon Mu's truth, I grit my teeth, repaying every moment I've passed through the Plane of Soul to Hyeon Mu.

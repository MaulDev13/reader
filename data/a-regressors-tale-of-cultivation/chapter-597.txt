In other words, all techniques that twist the principles of the world through one's heart or attraction force.

Then what exactly are Immortal Arts that are specifically referred to as mantras (眞言/True Words)?

A mantra is essentially a 'collection of Immortal Arts.'

Weaving together a couple Immortal Arts at a minimum or as many as thousands, and unfolding them is what is called a mantra.

The Phenomena Extinguishing Mantra, the Flawless Mantra, and even [Wheel], which combines the two, all fall into this category.

'Even the creation of the Small Heavenly Domain used by the Thirty-Three Heavenly Resplendent Treasure Immortals was probably a mantra as well.'

Strictly speaking, even my Impermanence Sword falls under this classification.

The Impermanence Sword is a collection of Immortal Arts, starting from Filling the Heavens Governing View to all the ultimate intents of my Crystal Glass to Treading Sea raised to the domain of Immortal Arts.

However, since the Impermanence Sword is something that has fused with my Entering Heavens, I named it sword rather than mantra.

"Exactly. Mantras are an assembly of Immortal Arts. But have you ever considered it this way?"

"The existence of True Immortals inherently benefits the world. In one way or another, we are beings that 'give birth' to something within this world. 'The Impermanence Sword is already alive.'

They simply exist in a form that lives in fusion with True Immortals.

Within the cells of living organisms, there exist cells that absorb the energy provided by the cells and, in return, supply them with the glucose necessary for survival.

Originally, these were separate cells that existed outside of the cells, but at some point, they entered the cells and coexisted, eventually evolving into organelles—or so the theory goes.

'Mi...what was it again?'

In any case, Immortal Arts and the Impermanence Sword give me a similar impression.

They function as one of my organs, moving together with me and responding to my heart, but they are existences with their own separate life and heart.

That is what Immortal Arts are.

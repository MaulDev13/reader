Scratching his head at the question from Makli Yoeo, the Elder in charge of the Beast Stable of the Makli Clan, Makli Hyun-ah asks.

"Because I need it for something recently...I am looking for a low-level demon beast with some intelligence. Among the low-level ones, is there perhaps any demon beast that is smart? Intelligence equivalent to around a nine- or ten-year-old child is fine."

"Hmm, a demon beast with a good head... Demon beasts with good heads are usually fox or raccoon-dog demon beasts. Those fellows, though they are low-level demon beasts, have a human-like intelligence. Want to take a look?"

At Makli Yoeo's words, Makli Hyun-ah arrives at the iron cage inside the Beast Stable where a raccoon-dog demon beast is confined.

"Huh...?"

Looking at the demon beast inside, Makli Hyun-ah is dumbfounded.

The raccoon-dog demon beast is wearing a modified hanbok and a splendid official hat as if it is a human.

Just like that bear demon beast!

"How is it? It is a raccoon-dog demon beast we captured recently, and its intelligence is quite high. Will you use this one?"

"First...can it talk?"

"Raccoon-dogs are good at human speech. Try talking to it."

"Um...hello? Raccoon-dog? What is your name?"

At those words, the hanbok-wearing demon beast glares at Makli Hyun-ah and speaks.

"There is a misunderstanding. I am not a raccoon-dog, I am a badger. I am telling you that you have captured the wrong being!"

"...? You are a badger?"

Makli Hyun-ah looks at the creature that claims it is a badger with a choked 'keek' sound and asks Makli Yoeo, but Makli Yoeo simply sighs and goes off somewhere else.

"It is a raccoon-dog that firmly believes it is a badger. I have to feed the other ones, so if you like that one, come find me."

"Uh, yes... Hey, they say you are a raccoon-dog."

"Keeeek, please do not say such ridiculous things! Would I, who am a badger, know better, or would you know better?"

—Makli Hyun-jok.

"Uh, Jegal Mikhail?"

Suddenly hearing Jegal Mikhail's voice calling him, Makli Hyun-ah pricks up his ears, and at his next words, he lets out a hollow laugh.

—That guy is not a raccoon-dog.

"Uh, then is this guy really a badger? But no matter how I look, it looks like a raccoon-dog..."

"You are the ones who are mistaken! I am a badger!"

—Makli Hyun-jok...that guy is neither a badger nor a raccoon-dog.

"Then what is it?"

—That guy is a raccoon...

"Ah..."

Looking at the raccoon demon beast he thought was a raccoon-dog that claims to be a badger, Makli Hyun-ah lets out a hollow laugh.

'This punk, who doesn't even know whether it's a raccoon or a badger...its intelligence seems a bit low. Will it really help with the Confucian Family Formation Method?'

Makli Hyun-ah seriously ponders how much help this stupid raccoon demon beast will be to the formation.

If I miss this chance, the next opportunity won't come until 100 years later.

Participating in a competition with Nascent Soul stage demon beasts without any cultivation in just 6 months?

But to do that, I need to deeply infiltrate the Dragon Race society within 100 years.

Just then.

"Of course, she is doing very well. Yuan Li was also someone who had assimilated Seo Hweol's blood, and I had forced him to reveal the method of assimilating Seo Hweol's blood through torture.
